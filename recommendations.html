Recommended Next Steps

Action items for leadership to address this incident

Immediate Action Required
The following steps should be completed as soon as possible to protect company interests.

Immediate Actions

URGENT
  • Block employee access to all company systems
    Disable Active Directory account, revoke VPN access, block email
  • Revoke all active sessions
    Force sign-out from all devices and applications
  • Collect company equipment
    Laptop, phone, badges, keys, and any other company property
  • Secure all accounts
    Change shared passwords, remove from shared accounts

Legal & HR Actions

HIGH PRIORITY
  • Review with Legal counsel
    Discuss potential legal implications and next steps
  • Document in personnel file
    Add investigation findings to employee record
  • Finalize employment termination
    Complete termination paperwork and process
  • Assess potential legal action
    Evaluate if civil action is warranted for data destruction
  • Review non-disclosure agreements
    Remind employee of ongoing confidentiality obligations

Business Recovery

RECOVERY
  • Recover deleted emails if needed
    454 emails can still be restored from Recoverable Items
  • Review recovered content
    Assess what business-critical information was affected
  • Redistribute critical information
    Ensure important emails reach the right people
  • Update access controls
    Review and update permissions for affected systems
  • Notify affected parties
    Inform relevant stakeholders of any data loss

Prevention Measures

LONG-TERM
  • Review employee offboarding procedures
    Ensure access is revoked before employees know of termination
  • Implement earlier email monitoring
    Set up alerts for unusual deletion activity
  • Update data retention policies
    Ensure critical emails are backed up automatically
  • Consider proactive litigation holds
    Apply holds before termination for high-risk employees
  • Train management on data security
    Educate on recognizing and reporting suspicious activity
